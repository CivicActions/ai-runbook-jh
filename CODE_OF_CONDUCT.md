# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [CivicActions Code of Conduct](https://civicactions.com/code-of-conduct/)
and the [Contributor Covenant v2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## Our Pledge

We are committed to making participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone,
regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics,
gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality,
personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:
- Using welcoming and inclusive language
- Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences
- Gracefully accepting constructive criticism
- Focusing on what is best for the community

Examples of unacceptable behavior:
- Harassment, trolling, or insulting/derogatory comments
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission
- Any conduct reasonably considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported to
jack.haas@civicactions.com. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly.
